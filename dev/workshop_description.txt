[h1]Spellbook[/h1]
Unlimited spell storage, right in the inventory's own spell row.

Every spell you pick up goes into the spellbook. Open the inventory and the spell row shows one page of it, as real cards: drag a spell into a wand to take one copy, and drag a spell from a wand into the row to store it. There are no new controls to learn.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Unlimited storage.[/b] Spells you pick up are always stored, even with the inventory closed.
[*][b]A table of contents.[/b] The SPELLBOOK panel lists the pages: a line per spell type, in the game's own order, with every spell you have unlocked shown faded where you have none. Rest the mouse on a page to look at it, click to select it. Tick "Merge categories" for one list, or "Hide unowned" to see only your own spells.
[*][b]Copies stack.[/b] One slot per spell, with a count of how many you have. Drag one out and the next copy takes its place.
[*][b]Limited-use spells.[/b] A slot shows the copy with the most uses left (or the fewest, in the settings). Right-click a spell to see every copy of it, one per slot, and take exactly the one you want.
[*][b]Search[/b] spells by name.
[*][b]Store all.[/b] Store every spell in your held wand, or in all your wands, with one click; double-click a spell in a wand to store just that one. These work where wands can be edited.
[*]Spell refreshers refill stored spells too.
[*]The panel can be dragged anywhere by its title bar, and collapsed to just the title bar.
[*]Everything is saved with your run.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
In the Mods menu: Merge categories, Hide unowned spells, Show locked spells, which copy of a limited-use spell shows first, the right-click hint, Reset panel position, and a debug log.

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Works with spells added by other mods, in any load order: the spell list is read once a run has started.
[*]Turn off other spell storage mods (such as Advanced Spell Inventory) to avoid conflicts.
[*]Something wrong? Turn on "Debug log" in the mod settings, do it again, then save and quit, and post the [i]spellbank2_debug_log[/i] value from [i]save00/world_state.xml[/i] (in %USERPROFILE%/AppData/LocalLow/Nolla_Games_Noita) with a description of what happened.
[/list]
