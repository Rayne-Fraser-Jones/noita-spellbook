[b]Unlimited spell storage for Noita, right in the inventory's own spell row.[/b]

No new controls to learn: the spell row shows a page of your spells as real cards, and you move them with the game's own drag-and-drop.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[b]Every spell, stored.[/b] Spells you pick up go straight into the spellbook, even with the inventory closed. Drag one into a wand to take it; drag it back to store it.

[b]A table of contents.[/b] The SPELLBOOK panel lists every page by spell type. Sweep the mouse down it to flick through the pages; click one to stay there.

[b]Copies stack.[/b] One slot per spell, with a count. Take one and the next copy takes its place.

[b]Pick the exact copy.[/b] Right-click a limited-use spell to see every copy of it by uses left.

[b]Search.[/b] Type part of a name to find a spell.

[b]Store all.[/b] In a Holy Mountain, store a whole wand in one click, or double-click a spell to store just that one.

Also: spell refreshers refill stored spells, the panel can be moved and collapsed, and everything is saved with your run.

[h2]Notes[/h2]
Turn off other spell storage mods, such as Advanced Spell Inventory. Spells added by other mods work, in any load order.
